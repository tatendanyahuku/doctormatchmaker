The Patient Dashboard serves as the primary interface for individuals seeking medical consultations, allowing them to search for doctors, book appointments, negotiate consultation fees, and engage in video calls or chat sessions. On the other hand, the Doctor Dashboard provides an interface for medical professionals to manage incoming patient requests, accept or decline request, conduct consultations, and prescribe medications. Both dashboards operate independently but remain interconnected through the backend, ensuring real-time synchronization of booking requests, payment processing, and consultation details.

The platform features horizontal navigation bars tailored to the role of each user. The Patient Dashboard Navbar includes options such as "Find a Doctor", "Upcoming Consultations", "Messages", "Prescriptions", "Wallet", and "Profile". The Doctor Dashboard Navbar has options such as "Pending Requests", "Scheduled Consultations", "Messages", "Earnings", "Prescriptions( where a prescription must contain doctors electronic signature for auntenticity so that patient can use the online prescription to get medication at pharmacy", and "Profile". These navigation bars enable quick access to key functionalities without unnecessary complexity. Both patients and doctors have access to the Profile section, where they can edit their personal information, including their name, contact details, and profile picture. Doctors can also update their specialization, consultation availability and qualifications.

The booking process is designed to allow patients to find and book doctors efficiently. After the patient has clicked the search doctor button, they select type of doctor they want like dermatologist or cardiologist then propose consultation fee and if the fee is below then minimum range they are prompted to raise their fee. Each specialty of doctors must have a fee range to avoid exploitation like cardiologist fee from $80 to $150 and dermatologists $50 to $120. Patient can only bid withing this range and the pool of doctors in the specified specialty can see the offer and accept if they are interested in the offer. All doctors who would have accepted the fee are displayed to the patient then the patient accepts the doctor they want according to their rating and others are automatically rejected after selection. Once they find a suitable doctor, they can initiate a booking request by describing their symptoms, selecting a consultation type (video call, chat, or both), and proposing date for their consultation. When patient is booking date of consultation the system must flag a consultation if there is another patient who would have booked on the same date and time allowing patients to book for a free slot. Upon agreement, the patient is redirected to the payment page, where they can pay via wallet balance, credit/debit card, or insurance(provide insurance claims form). After payment, the appointment is confirmed and added to both the patient’s and doctor’s dashboards under "Scheduled Consultations". The doctor must be able to view waiting room on a daily bases where the see the patients and time they scheduled. 

The video calling and messaging system allows for smooth online consultations. At the scheduled appointment time, both the patient and doctor can join the consultation room through their respective dashboards. The system initiates a WebRTC-based video call, ensuring real-time audio and video communication. For patients who prefer text-based consultations, a chat window allows them to exchange messages with the doctor. All messages are stored in the SQLite database, making it possible to retrieve past conversations. After the consultation, doctors can generate a digital prescription, which is stored under the "Prescriptions" tab for both parties. Patients can then download their prescriptions as a file.

The platform ensures data consistency by leveraging SQLite as the in-app database. Each user type has a dedicated table in the database, with separate tables for patients, doctors, consultations, messages, payments, and prescriptions. When a patient books an appointment, the consultation details are stored in the consultations table, and the doctor’s dashboard fetches pending appointments dynamically. Profile edits are immediately reflected in the database, ensuring that both doctors and patients always have updated information. Additionally, earnings and wallet balances are tracked for doctors, allowing them to withdraw funds when needed.
